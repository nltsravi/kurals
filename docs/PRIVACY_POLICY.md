## 1. Overview
NLTS Ravi developed the **திருக்குறள் (Thirukkural)** mobile app as a free, open-source, and ad-free educational tool. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as-is.

This Privacy Policy informs users regarding policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.

## 2. Information Collection and Use
**We do NOT collect, transmit, sell, or share ANY personal information.**

- **No User Registration:** You can use all features of the app without providing your name, email, phone number, or any credential.
- **No Analytics / Telemetry:** No third-party tracking frameworks (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel) are embedded.
- **No Advertisements:** The app contains zero ads and zero advertising SDKs (e.g. AdMob).
- **No Device Identifiers:** The app does not access hardware serial numbers, IMEI, or advertising IDs.

## 3. Permissions Requested
The application is configured with `permissions: []` in `app.json`.
- **Location:** Not requested.
- **Camera / Audio:** Not requested.
- **Contacts / Phone:** Not requested.
- **Storage:** Only used locally through the standard Android system share sheet when you choose to export your collections (TXT, JSON, DOCX).
- **Network:** Only used if you tap external links (Rate on Google Play, GitHub repository). The complete dataset of 1,330 couplets is bundled locally for 100% offline access.

## 4. Local Data Storage
Bookmarks and custom collections created within the app are saved exclusively on your local device storage using standard application sandboxing (`AsyncStorage`). This data never leaves your device and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app or clear app data.

## 5. Google Play Data Safety Compliance
- **Data collected:** None
- **Data shared:** None
- **Tracking:** None
- **Security:** Data stays inside the sandboxed operating system environment.

## 6. Children's Privacy
The app does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13. It is rated for all ages (IARC 3+ / Everyone).
