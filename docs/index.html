Google Play "No Data Collected" Verified

This app does not collect, track, share, or transmit ANY user or device data. All 1,330 couplets run 100% offline directly from your device.

1. Overview and Commitment

NLTS Ravi built the திருக்குறள் (Thirukkural) mobile application as a free, open-source, and ad-free educational utility for Tamil and English readers worldwide. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is designed from the ground up to respect user privacy completely.

This Privacy Policy explains that our application operates entirely offline and does not harvest any sensitive personal information, device identifiers, or usage telemetry.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal data whatsoever. When you download and use the Thirukkural app:

  • No Account Required: You are not required to create an account, register, or provide an email address, name, or phone number.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: The app contains zero analytics SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, or third-party trackers).
  • No Advertisements: The app is 100% ad-free (no AdMob, Facebook Audience Network, or any advertisement frameworks).
  • No Device Identifiers: We do not collect IMEI, MAC address, Android Advertising ID (AAID), or hardware serial numbers.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Access

The app is intentionally configured with zero dangerous or invasive permissions in AndroidManifest / app.json:

Permission Status Explanation
Location (GPS) NOT REQUESTED We never access your physical location.
Camera / Microphone NOT REQUESTED We never capture photos, audio, or video.
Contacts / Phone State NOT REQUESTED We never read your address book or call logs.
External Storage OPTIONAL EXPORT ONLY Only when you explicitly choose to export your bookmarked collections as TXT, JSON, or DOCX via the system Share Sheet.
Internet / Network OPTIONAL The complete dataset of 1,330 couplets is locally bundled. Internet is only triggered if you tap external links (e.g. rate on Play Store or GitHub).

4. Local On-Device Storage

When you bookmark a Kural or create a custom collection, this information is stored locally on your device using standard local secure storage (AsyncStorage). This data is completely private to you, never leaves your smartphone or tablet, and is automatically erased if you choose to uninstall the application.

5. Google Play Data Safety Questionnaire Summary

For Google Play Console Data Safety compliance:

  • Data shared: None. No user data is shared with any third party.
  • Data collected: None. The app does not collect user data.
  • Security practices: All local user bookmarks remain confined within the operating system's app sandbox.
  • Data deletion: All local preferences are wiped upon clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

6. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13 specifically, nor do we knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. The application is completely suitable and safe for all ages (IARC 3+ / Everyone).
